**Short version for children:** everything you do in Pip's World stays on this device.

## What we collect

Nothing. Pip's World has no accounts and no sign-in, and it doesn't send anything to us or to anyone else.

## What stays on the device

- Progress: stars, levels completed, and how each skill is going (right answers, hints used).
- Settings chosen by a grown-up: language, age band, daily play time, sound, captions, reduced motion, bilingual
  mode, extra-large buttons, microphone on/off.
- A child's nickname and avatar, if you create a profile (no real names needed).
- Counts of games left unfinished, shown to you on the progress page to help choose activities.

All of this is stored only on this device. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Microphone

The microphone is off until a grown-up turns it on in the grown-up area (behind a grown-up check). When Pip repeats
what your child says, the sound is held in memory for a few seconds, played back once in a funny voice, then
discarded. It is never written to storage and never sent anywhere. The app works fully with the microphone off.

## No ads, analytics or tracking

There are no adverts, no analytics or attribution services, no crash-reporting services, and no tracking of any kind.
Our build checks fail if such a service is ever added (`app/tool/audit_dependencies.dart`).

## Purchases

A one-time purchase unlocks all levels. It is handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see payment details.
Purchase buttons are only in the grown-up area.

## Children's privacy

Pip's World is made for children aged 2–7 and is designed to meet the Apple Kids Category and Google Play Families
requirements, COPPA and GDPR-K: no personal data is collected, so there is nothing to share, sell or delete from a
server.
